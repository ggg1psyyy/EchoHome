# Rooting

> **You unlock an Echo at your own risk.** A step gone wrong can soft-brick it;
> EchoMuse's guide covers recovering it.

Unlocking an Echo, choosing its target and what the setup wizard then writes
are documented by EchoMuse, whose unlock path and wizard EchoHome uses:
**[EchoMuse's rooting guide](https://github.com/wilbowes/EchoMuse/blob/main/docs/rooting.md)**.

EchoHome is built on [EchoMuse](https://github.com/wilbowes/EchoMuse) by Wil Bowes and the EchoMuse contributors. The unlocks are R0rt1z2's (amonet-biscuit, amonet-radar);
[firebreak](https://github.com/bboe/firebreak) by bboe runs them.
